Texture Shift

Import items brings textures in and turns them into Minecraft blocks, plants and resource packs. Edit textures merges a new look into batches of textures, part by part. Both use the same library of imported textures.

1 · Add textures

Import a texture pack, a folder or loose images into the library. Then tap textures to select them and add them to the Minecraft pack below.

Library

What gets imported
  • Packs: .zip, .mcpack, .mcaddon, Java resource packs and .jar files, including packs inside packs.
  • Inside a pack only the block folders are read (assets/…/textures/block/ for Java, textures/blocks/ for Bedrock). Mobs, items, particles, menus, weather and sky are skipped.
  • Images must be square, 8–1024 px, a power of two (16, 32, 64…). A tall strip of square frames is an animation and its first frame is used.
  • Stairs, fences, doors, rails, torches, plants and other non-cube shapes and mostly-transparent cut-outs are skipped when you edit textures. On Import items they come in too (untick the box to skip them). Glass, leaves and ice always import.
  • A plain image or a zip of plain images, with no pack folders, is accepted when it is square and the right size.
  • Everything skipped is listed with its reason, and most of it can be imported anyway.
